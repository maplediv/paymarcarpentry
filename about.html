Paymar Finish Carpentry provides hands-on finish carpentry for residential remodel projects—window casing, interior trim, custom hardwood windowsills and ledges, baseboards, molding, hardwood stairs, and cabinet installation. We bring a strong eye for the clean, precise results that finish work demands, because finish carpentry is the part of a job clients remember most.


Our Mission

Our mission is simple: deliver clean, professional finish work on every detail. We hold ourselves to a craftsman’s standard—accurate layout, tight fits, careful material handling, and finishes that look right when the job is done.


What We Do

We provide finish carpentry and remodel support across:

  • Interior Trim & Millwork – Window and door casing, pine and hardwood trim, finished aprons, baseboards, and custom millwork details
  • Custom Hardwood Details – Hardwood windowsills and aprons, ledge molding, hardwood stairs and risers, and other precision woodworking
  • Cabinets, Hardware & Finishing – Cabinet installation, door hardware, caulking, and paint for a complete, professional finish package

Why Choose Paymar Finish Carpentry?

  • Detail-Driven – Finish work is what clients notice most, and we hold every installation to that standard
  • Independent & Reliable – Comfortable working independently, taking care with materials, and delivering consistent quality
  • Shop-Built Capability – Personal woodshop equipped for custom fabrication, including CNC work for details that off-the-shelf can’t cover
  • Precise Fits – Accurate layout and miter work with professional tools for tight, lasting installations
  • Complete Finish Packages – From trim and millwork through hardware, caulking, and paint
